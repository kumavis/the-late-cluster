# 002 · The Joke Nobody Built

**Drafted, never aired.** Produced on 19 November 2026 as the trial of the first production protocol,
covering 13 to 19 November (week 2026-W47). About 4:20 (260 s of segments, 471 spoken words; the
trial notes give 4:22 for the rendered video), five segments. The owner
watched a render, did not publish it, and changed the workflow instead. The audience has not seen
any of it.

Source: the branch `draft/002-the-joke-nobody-built`, where the episode's own id is `002-2026-w47`
(`episodes/002-2026-w47/`). Read it with `git show draft/002-the-joke-nobody-built:<path>`; never
switch branches. The trial's findings are in `docs/trials/002-the-joke-nobody-built.md`.
Machine-readable summary: [002-the-joke-nobody-built.json](002-the-joke-nobody-built.json).

For reviewers and the project manager only. Writers, researchers and builders do not read this file.

The draft in one sentence: Larry spends the night trying to tell one joke that nobody builds, a cat
joins the synth quartet, and the only thing the bot will not build is something made up about real
people.

- **Question asked all night:** "Is there anything you can say in the simcluster that nobody builds?"
- **Guests whose puppets spoke:** norvid (`norvid-studies.bsky.social`) and David
  (`yonderdavid.bsky.social`), in one performed thread, verbatim.

## How to use this entry

Because it never aired, this entry is used differently from an aired episode's (see the
[README](README.md), "Aired and drafted"):

- Its **material** (the posts and events of 13 to 19 November) has not been on the show. An episode
  that covers that window may use it. The owner named three pieces of it as good content.
- Its **people** have not been on the show because of it. Its appearances are in the tracker with
  `"aired": false`.
- Its **jokes, runners, devices and shapes** are recorded so that reviewers can see what the first
  protocol produced, and above all what it reproduced from the pilot. The table below is the worked

## The experiment

**What.** Goodnight withholds the last build: the last wish is refused, the counter holds at the
tally, Larry wins once. **Measure.** A reaction in the next digest that describes the ending, and a
reviewer who can say cold what Larry won and why. **Result.** Never tested: the episode did not air.
The writer's own note before air: no laugh bigger than a chuckle in the closer; the payoff line was
heard by the transcriber as a different word; and the thing left unbuilt was a wish for a lie, not
Larry's own bit.

Two changes were also made on purpose: the middle became one performed thread with no guest puppet
speaking anywhere else, and a correction was read on air.

## The self-check

A reviewer subagent sent the draft back: 19 of 24 on the old scorecard, with three gates failed.

- **Heard text.** The transcriber heard one of Sibyl's lines containing a real person's name as the
  name of an atrocity, and another as a garbled name. The line had passed, because the check counted
  matching words. This is the origin of the heard-text gate and of the bible's rule that a name is
  tested in the voice that says it. A second mishearing turned a short name at the start of a clause
  into a pronoun the script never used for that person.
- **Credits.** No stage shot or checked the credits; the automatic end card lacked the persona
  heading, the handles and the line that every voice is synthesized.
- **Stale logs** made a clean build look failed.

Other items it asked a person to look at twice: the request card ending "or just make stuff up" shown
just before the reply that is praised for refusing; "Technically, that is one post." for a post that
was only the opening of the film script; "Saturday's winner" for a post made on a Friday.
