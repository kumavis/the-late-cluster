**Aired.** 13 November 2026, week 2026-W46. 9:51, about 900 spoken words, eight segments.
Source: `episodes/001-pilot/` (`segments/*/script.js` for the words, `index.js` for the staging).

For reviewers and the project manager only. Writers, researchers and builders do not read this file.

The pilot in one sentence: a post asks for a simcluster talk show as a joke, a bot builds it, and for
ten minutes everything anyone wishes for gets built while the host keeps asking what the simcluster is.

- **Question asked all night:** "What is the simcluster?" Answered at the close by two real posts.
- **Guests whose puppets spoke:** Samantha (`samantha.wiki`), norvid (`norvid-studies.bsky.social`),
  Abel (`abeliansoup.bsky.social`), BorJev (`borjev.bsky.social`, a cameo).
- **Song:** "Someday (Built It)", a stomp-clap anthem.
- **Things "built" on the counter:** 15.

### The eleven "Built it." shapes

The first bible catalogued these as shapes to rotate. All eleven are done.

| shape | how | where |
|---|---|---|
| plain | a wish, a beat, "Built it." | cold open; monologue ("This monologue was a joke an hour ago.") |
| pre-empted | someone else has already built it | Hy: "Already built it, Larry." |
| mid-sentence | it ships while the line is still going | weather ("It shipped during that sentence..") |
| interrupting | it overlaps the ruling | game show ("Bit," / "Built it." / "She didn't even finish!") |
| protested | a character objects | sports ("Hey! That was hypothetical!") |
| physical | the built thing arrives in the studio | the E key; the crow out of the machine |
| on the host | the build happens to Larry | Larry in a museum case |
| doubled | one wish, two builds | song ("Built it." / "Both of them?") |
| the reply | the bot answers a question with a fact | song ("When?" / "You are in it.") |
| contradicting | the tally is wrong the moment it is read | goodnight (fourteen, then a fifteenth) |
| useless | it builds exactly what was asked, and it does not help | goodnight (the gn checker, which says NO) |

The first bible also proposed as "new shapes": built too early, built wrong, built by the audience,
and the bot refusing on ethics. The trial draft used the last one (see 002).

## Where the pilot breaks today's rules

The pilot was made before the constitution's Article 3 and several of chapter 9's rules. It is a
record, not a model. The constitution (R3, R4, R6, R7, R8, R12) cites some of these as its own worked
examples; they are collected here so that a reviewer can find them in one place.

- **Invented words in real people's mouths (R3).** norvid: "concept: this interview, but it's a museum
  exhibit," and "concept: talk show, but a giant crow hunts the host,"; "abel?". Abel: "they don't want
  you to know this but (it's already running)". Samantha: the whole forecast patter, "Hundred
  percent." and "It shipped during that sentence..".
- **Real phrases made to answer what they were not answering (R4).** "claim." as a reply to "Thank you
  for coming" and as a sign-off; "what else is like this" as an answer to "what is the simcluster?";
  "good news, everyone!" as a reply to norvid.
- **A paraphrase in a real person's voice.** Abel's "my bot. opt-in only now."
- **One staged exchange that passes** (the owner's ruling): *buddy that's my freaking simcluster* as a
  reply to "My beautiful city".
- **Typography lost in speech.** Abel's "(like an alligator) (respectfully)": the parentheses are the
  joke and the respelling strips them.
- **Invented handles on real top-level domains (R8).** Listed above. The house chyron handles were
  later removed from the studio.

## Analysis: what worked and what did not

Moved from the first bible's joke chapter. Evidence for reviewers; never to be quoted to a writer.

**The strongest jokes, and why.**
1. "She founded the chicken. She cannot be the chicken." A real rule that disqualifies its own
   founder; parallel construction with one word changed; staged (the bonk). Specific, affectionate.
2. "So now there are four Discords." The rule of the setup defeats itself, and a real post tops it.
3. "Both weeks were on Monday." A real habit with a factual twist, tagged on screen.
4. Exhibit 31. The joke is all in the staging and the placard.
5. The song's stops: "Both of them?" / "When?" / "You are in it." The song's form is the joke's form.
6. "Two to three inches of assigned reading." Genre vocabulary on community content, with a picture.
   (Spoken by a guest's puppet, which is no longer allowed.)
7. "Hey! That was hypothetical!" The one character who objects to the runner.

**The weaker jokes, and why.**
- "I scored a sixty one. / My mom says I'm a hundred." A generic template; the site's real footer was
  the better material.
- "Vibes remain immeasurable." Connective tissue.
- "Even the crow came down for this." Filler over a picture that was already the joke.
- "And we didn't even play upping my p(doom)." Rests on a reference the episode never set up; rescued
  by the board.
- "Simcluster Hamilton!" / "That took four seconds!" Restates the runner.
- "A hot dog toppings tier list!" Not known to be from the cluster, so the format's point is lost.
- Meta three times: the cold open, "Wait. It's this show." and "You are in it."

**Habits worth knowing when judging a later episode.** A reaction sound after nearly every line (ten
big laughs, three rimshots). About fifteen stamps. A picture on almost every punch word. Screen-only
jokes on nearly every graphic. Fifteen builds in ten minutes, which the community noticed as weight
(see Reactions).
