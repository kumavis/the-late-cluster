(`docs/WORKFLOW.md`): research on Opus, ideas and scripts on Fable, the repetition gate, and
fitting by a sound editor. This is not an episode. The drafts may be selected into one later.

1. **The Riemann hypothesis.** On 20 November 2026 a mathematician announced that, working with

2. **A "making of" The Late Cluster.** An explainer of how the show is made that is recursive: it
   explains the making of the making-of, and so on down, and it is staged in three dimensions,
   each level of the recursion a place the camera can travel into.

What the owner's commission changes, for these two scripts only:
- The bible's limit of one joke about the show itself per episode does not apply to the second
  script. The show is its subject, by the owner's choice.
- Everything in the constitution applies as always: real people say only what they published, in
  its context; nothing invented is attributed to anyone; the out-of-bounds subjects stay out.
