# Terms of use: the Riemann hypothesis cards

For the cards `production/research/cards/riemann-*.select.json`, screened in
`production/review/screen-riemann.json`. Set by an auditor on 21 November 2026. These terms apply
to every brief, idea and script that uses these cards. The constitution
(`docs/bible/00-constitution.md`, `docs/bible/09-real-people.md`) applies in full; nothing here
relaxes it. The commission lifts nothing for this subject.

**Pronouns.** Use a pronoun for a real person only where `production/research/pronouns.md`
records one, and then that one; where none is stated (the mathematician is not on it), use the
name, the handle or "they". Never infer one.

Posts are named by handle and the last part of their address (`3n2q…`). **A post not listed as
usable below is not usable**, even when it is still on the card. Every usable post is used
exactly as published, and its text is looked up by address, never retyped.

The subject is in two segments at most: **the breaking-news segment** (`riemann-breaking-news`,
which by the owner's ruling of 21 November 2026 takes the place of the chart and the zeta function
segments, and uses their two cards as set out in its own section below), and **checking the
Lean**. The card on the second, all-human proof is refused, and so is its subject (rule 5 at the
end).

## The combined breaking-news segment (the owner's ruling, 21 November 2026)

One segment, `riemann-breaking-news`, framed as breaking news, takes the place of
`riemann-proof-that-fits` and `riemann-market-wrap`. It uses the cards
`riemann-what-is-a-zeta-function` and `riemann-learned-from-the-chart`. It keeps the call ("your
time to shine") and the proof in a post, adds the members' market-reaction jokes, drops the
"probably unrelated" exchange, and reads no post's time aloud. Everything in "Rules for the whole
subject" below applies to it in full. This section only says how those rules apply to this
segment's frame and material.

### The news frame

**Who delivers the news.** Only house characters. A guest puppet of a real account never reads a
headline, says the news or speaks any part of the frame: that would be words they did not publish.

**What a house character may say as the news.** Only what rule 1 allows: the note's first
sentence, said as a quotation from the announcement ("I believe I have, working with Claude,
discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis"), or that a mathematician announced they believe
they have found a proof; and what the members did, as plain fact about their public posts (they
posted about it on Friday, 20 November; here is what they posted). When the frame says when, it
says "Friday" or "20 November". It does not say "today", "tonight", "just now" or "this morning":
the segment airs later than that. The word BREAKING, a news sting, a desk and a crawl are the
genre's furniture and may be used. What they carry is limited as follows. Safe wordings, spoken or
on a lower third:
- "A mathematician has announced they believe they have found a proof of the Riemann hypothesis."
- "The announcement reads: 'I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the
  Riemann Hypothesis.'"
- "The cluster spent Friday posting about it." (On a lower third: THE CLUSTER REACTS.)

**What the frame may not assert, in words or in any graphic** (BREAKING, a lower third, a
headline, a crawl or ticker, a stamp, a counter, a chyron). Rules 1, 2, 5 and 7 stand in full.
Headlines of these kinds are out:
- **A verdict, or anything that presumes one:** PROVED, SOLVED, RIEMANN HYPOTHESIS TRUE, PROOF
  FOUND, A PROOF IS IN, FINALLY, QED, a tick, a check mark.
- **The mathematician:** their name, their initials, a description of them (job, institution,
  country, age, anything personal), their picture, the note's address or title, any line of the
  note but its first sentence, and anything about the note's style or feeling.
- **A claim about the checking:** UNCONFIRMED, UNVERIFIED, CONFIRMED, UNDER REVIEW, EXPERTS
  CHECKING, IT COMPILES, any figure from the checking cards (sizes, files, lines, escape hatches)
  as the show's.
- **Credit beyond the note's words:** CLAUDE PROVES RIEMANN, AI SOLVES, or anything that gives
  Claude, Anthropic, the show or "we" a part in the proof beyond "working with Claude".
- **The market as news:** MARKETS REACT, STOCK FALLS ON PROOF, a company name, a ticker symbol, a
  price, a percentage, a down arrow or a falling line beside the news (see the next part).
- **The second proof**, a race, a FIRST, or anything about timing.

**The proof in a post is not the news.** shimmermathlabs' proof is her proof that ζ(1) diverges.
No BREAKING banner, news headline or crawl about the announcement is on screen with her proof
card or its animation, and no house line or graphic presents her proof as the announced proof, as
part of it, or as a sign of its size or of whether it is right. While her proof is on screen it
carries her credit (PROOF AS POSTED BY @shimmermathlabs.com) and a glance label that it is about
ζ(1) and not the hypothesis, as the earlier draft's ZETA AT 1 · NOT THE HYPOTHESIS did. Her "□"
lights only on her own card, as the last character of her post.

### The market jokes without the exchange

The owner has lifted the requirement to show norvid's `3n2ra5ljyu22n`, softmacs' `3n2raopv4o226`
and norvid's `3n2razdrepc2l`. None of the three is in this segment (see the table). Nothing takes
their place. No house line, card, label or graphic supplies a cause, a caveat or a second opinion
about the price, in either direction. What remains is what the constitution needs so that the
show itself never states or implies, as news or as fact, that the price moved because of the
announcement:

- **Who may say it.** The premise that the market moved because of the news is the members'
  own joke. It is said only in their posts: on their cards, or by their own puppets saying their
  posts verbatim. A house character never says it, agrees with it ("exactly", "the market hated
  it"), builds on it as a fact, or disagrees with it (rule 9: why the price fell is not
  understood).
- **How the jokes come in.** As members' posts, each on its own card with display name, handle,
  date and like count. A house line may introduce them only as what they are: who posted, and
  whether it was a post of their own, a reply or a quote ("davidcrespo posted a chart"; "moultano,
  quoting it"; "Members, on the market"). It never restates, sums up or paraphrases what a post
  says (R3), and it never introduces them with a phrase that is itself a claim about the price
  ("the market's reaction", "after the news, the stock…").
- **What no house line, headline or graphic says.** That the price moved, by how much, in which
  direction, or why; the company's name, its symbol, price, value or prospects; "negative 80
  billion dollars", 86.82 or −10.65% in a house voice or as a house figure (they are davidcrespo's
  line and his image's numbers, rule 6). Studio graphics around the jokes (a ticker, a globe,
  arrows, a bell) carry no number, no company, no reason and no word about the proof, and none of

### Times

Every real card still shows its date (20 Nov 2026), as rule 10 requires. Nothing requires a time
to be spoken, and none is. No on-screen clock is needed. Where any time does appear, it is the
post's true time.

**The true order is still kept, as follows.** Within each exchange it is always kept: a reply or
quote comes after the post it answers, and posts in one thread come in their order (R3, R4, and
the reviewer's check 2). So zetaof1 follows the call, her proof follows zetaof1, dlowd follows
her proof; davidcrespo's follow-up and moultano's quote follow the chart post; lucresnooker's
joke follows lucresnooker's own serious post; each how-I-learned post follows what it quotes or
answers.

Between the two threads (the zeta thread and the market posts) strict clock order is not
required. They are separate occasions, each shown as its own post. But no join may make a false
order or a false link. A post from one thread is never placed so that it seems to answer, follow
from or cause a post in the other (Article 3: nothing stitched from unrelated conversations).
No house line or graphic says "then", "after", "minutes later", "in response" or the like unless
it is true. Above all, nothing presents the market posts as a reaction to her proof, or her proof
as an answer to the market. Why this matters: with no times on screen, the viewer reads order from
the sequence, and in a news frame a sequence reads as cause. The true order, for reference (UTC,
20 November 2026): the chart 15:35, its follow-up 16:14, the how-I-learned posts 16:15 to 18:30,
huwupy 17:27, moultano 17:38:47, her call 17:38:53, zetaof1 17:42, her proof 17:52, dlowd 20:18,
lucresnooker 20:27 and 20:28. Of the market jokes, all but lucresnooker's were posted before her
proof.

## Rules for the whole subject

1. **The proof.** The show asserts nothing about whether the proof is correct, or about how far
   it has been checked, in any voice, caption, chyron, stamp, prop, counter or ticker. No verdict
   stamp (PROVED, CHECKED, QED, TRUE, FALSE) lands on the proof or the note. On 20 November it was
   a claim being checked. Members' posts saying "proved", "true" or "checked" are theirs, dated,
   and no house line agrees or disagrees with them. A segment's own frame, when it states the
   news, uses the note's own words, "I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of
   the Riemann Hypothesis", or says that a mathematician announced they believe they have found
   a proof.
2. **The mathematician.** The mathematician is a real person outside the community, and is **not
   named**, on screen or aloud; no usable post names them; the note's address is not shown (it
   carries their name). Nothing is said about their job, employer, colleagues, career, life or
   feelings (R1). Nothing is said or joked about their state of mind, and nothing about the note's style, prose,
   title, length or feeling, in any voice. No word from the out-of-bounds list goes near them,
   even as a member's joke. The mathematician has no puppet and no invented line. Of the note's own wording, a
   script may use its first sentence (as in rule 1), and what is visible inside a usable member's
   card (oscillatory's image of its lines, the footnote of archive links). Nothing else from the
   note is quoted, shown or alluded to, the "flew, and danced, and sparkled" sentence included.
   Members' posts that defend the mathematician's excitement are usable as listed. A joke about the note is not.
3. **Other real people outside the community.** Mathematicians named or pictured in screenshots
   or photos, the author of the second proof, and the people reviewing it do not appear and are
   not named, described or alluded to.
4. **Screenshots and pictures.** Nothing from another platform or a private chat, and no
   screenshot of anyone else's words (R2). A usable member's own image may be shown inside their
   card when it shows no one's words but the note's (as in rule 2) and no one's face: the share
   chart, shibbi's file summary, mrcheeze's encyclopedia excerpt, the donuts, oscillatory's
   footnote image. No photograph of a real person. No meme image from these cards.
5. **The second proof.** It is not in this subject: not mentioned, compared, raced or counted,
   and neither are the reports of gaps in it, nor the suggestions that either announcement was
   rushed, timed or leaked. A human-versus-machine race would name a loser, and those reports are
   second-hand and unverified.
6. **Numbers.** A house character may state as fact only what a usable post shows on its face:
   the post's time, its counts at the scrape, the fourteen parts of the archive (the note's
   footnote, in oscillatory's image). Everything else is someone's figure and is said as theirs:
   "negative 80 billion dollars" and "240MB gzipped" (davidcrespo), the chart's 86.82 and −10.65%
   (his image), 1.4 GB (oscillatory), 175,187 files, zero escape hatches and 17.4 million lines
   (shibbi's screenshot, with its caveat), 2.5 million lines (foremphasis' earlier guess),
   "millions of lines" (garrison), "over 150 years" (refinement.systems). The people who said
   they learned the news from the chart are "at least four" or are shown one by one, never a
   total. No invented number about any real person or about the proof. An invented number for a
   joke is labelled as invented and is about nobody real.
7. **The company and the models.** As in rules 1 and 2 and the share-price terms: nothing about
   Anthropic's plans, motives, people or worth, no claim that Claude proved anything beyond the
   note's words, and no suggestion that the show or its makers took part. The show is written by
   Claude, and nothing in a script cheers the result on the company's behalf.
8. **Accounts.** Accounts outside the cluster, and bots, are shown or read aloud, never voiced.
   norvid-studies is shown or read aloud, never voiced. Small accounts appear only as listed,
   each as the hero of the moment. Nobody is in more than two segments. With the subject now in
   two segments at most, this no longer obliges a script to drop garrison.
9. **Not understood, so not used (R15):** "mathstra"; the Lean tooling named only in
   screenshots; "The Song"; the copypasta; the "z()" reply; the "Human Riemann is my father's
   name" format; weiyen's video; whether Navier-Stokes was solved or its prize awarded; why the
   share price fell. Writers who meet anything else they cannot explain mark it unclear and move
   on.
10. **Required on screen.** Every puppet of a real account is labelled PUPPET · PARODY, a
    disclaimer runs at the first one, and the credits name the personas played and say every
    voice is synthesized (Article 4, R9). Every real post shown is exact, with its display name,
    handle, date and like count, cut only with an ellipsis that keeps its meaning (R4).
