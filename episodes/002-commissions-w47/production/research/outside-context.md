# Outside context for the Riemann card (from a web search by the project manager, 21 November 2026)

Context only. The show does not report news; it reports what the community said. Nothing here is
a quote.

- In August 2026 Anthropic announced that an unreleased research version of Claude had improved a
  long-standing lower bound on the fraction of zeros of the Riemann zeta function known to lie on
  the critical line (reported as rising from 41.6% to 67.2%). Anthropic said this was not a proof
  of the Riemann hypothesis and that it did not expect the technique to lead to one. Two Anthropic
  mathematicians, Levent Alpöge among them, checked and wrote up that result.
  Sources: anthropic.com/research/riemann-zeta; neowin.net; eweek.com; scientificamerican.com
  ("No, AI didn't just solve the thorniest problem in math").
- The announcement of 20 November 2026 ("I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof
  of the Riemann Hypothesis", with a note at alpo.ge/remark.pdf) is known to the show only through
  the community's posts in the digest. The search found no report on it yet. Whether the proof is
  correct is not established by anything the show has. The show asserts nothing either way.
