# Audit: the redraft of making-of-inward against its terms

Brief: `briefs/screen/auditor-making-of-redraft.md`. Read against `research/terms-making-of.md`,
`research/production-facts.md` (with its section "The pipeline in one line"),
`research/pronouns.md`, the constitution, and the earlier findings in `review/audit-drafts.md` and
`review/audit-drafts-2.md`. The script is `drafts/making-of-inward/script.js` as of 21 November
2026, 14:09.

Verdict: **change**

There is one `must`. Everything else passes, and the notes are for the owner, the stager and the
sound editor.

## 1. Facts

- **must** (to the writer). The label over the desks at cue `throat-two`, "the last run's count:
  9 IN · 6 OUT". The sheet's sentences are "9 segments drafted, about 745 seconds, against a
  480-second target" and "6 segments selected". "OUT" can be read as "six cut", and that gives
  three kept. It is drawn over pages of which "Some go on up. Some drop away." So the label
  states a number a viewer can read the wrong way round. It also carries nothing to say it is the
  last run's count, so it reads as this episode's. The terms say a number "is used only as the fact
  sheet states it ... never something a viewer could take for a record". Any of these satisfies
  it: a label whose second word can only mean "passed" (selected, through, kept, or the like);
  the label spoken in a line; or cutting the label. If the label stays, the picture should show
  six pages going on and three dropping, so that it agrees with the label.
- **note** (for the owner). `review-early-seen`: "These reviewers have seen everything the show has
  done. Nothing gets in twice." This rests on the sheet's stage 6: "Two reviewers who have read
  everything the show has aired block any idea it has already done". The sheet goes on: "At most
  one item an episode may instead turn an old one on its head." "Nothing gets in twice" is the
  rule without its one exception. A reversal is not the same thing getting in twice, so this is a
  fair shorthand and not a breach. `review-early-except` ("Except review.") is plainly wordplay on
  the gauge and states no fact.
- **note** (for the owner, carried over from `audit-drafts.md`). `l2-list`: "The show is not on it.
  By rule." The sheet says "Writers never read earlier episodes or the record of what the show has
  done". The line is true only if "the show" is heard as "the show's episodes". The line is
  unchanged, only moved, so the earlier note still stands.
- **note** (for the owner). The 248 lights at `cluster` come straight after `posts-count`
  ("twenty thousand, eight hundred and twenty-five"). The sheet says those posts came "from 2,786
  accounts: the members and the people they talked with". Nothing says the 248 wrote them all, and
  the lights carry no label. A viewer might still put the two together. This is not a breach.
- Every other line that states a process fact matches the sheet:
  - The pipeline. The gauge's seven words, the five titles (`scenes-title`, `review-late-title`,
    `scripts-title`, `review-early-title`, `posts-title`), `bottom-community`, and `up-community`
    to `up-show` together give the owner's line, "community, then posts, then review, then
    scripts, then review, then scenes, then the show". The dive reads it backwards, which the
    sheet allows: "the same line read backwards is what each thing was made of". Each level's
    content matches the sheet's mapping:
    - scenes: "builders stage the scenes (11)";
    - the later review: "reviewers and auditors select and check them (9 and 10)". `review-late-rule`
      ("Reviewers choose. Whoever makes a thing does not judge it.") is the sheet's "Whoever makes a
      thing does not judge it" and "Reviewers score the drafts";
    - scripts: "writers write scripts (7 and 8)";
    - the earlier review: "reviewers rank them and pick ideas (3 to 6)";
    - posts: "the scraper and researchers turn posts into cards (stages 1 and 2)".
  - `l0-parts`: "jaw, tongue, lips, velum".
  - The hanging frames in throat one: "A segment's staging is checked by shooting still frames at
    every cue and looking at them".
  - `scenes-builders`: "Builders write the pictures" and "A builder's report on staging a
    segment".
  - The two back-to-back desks: "judged by at least two reviewers working independently".
  - `scripts-writer`: "The work is done by AI agents, each given a role, a brief and a short
    reading list".
  - The door plate: "Its own source describes it as 'a guard, not a wall'".
  - `scripts-kazoo`: "A tool renders every line, transcribes it" and "it heard 'kazoo' as
    'clue'".
  - `posts-count` and its counter: "The scrape held 20,825 posts", from the last full run, and the

  - The 248 lights: "248 accounts in the cluster".

  - The nesting, the smaller Larrys, the shelves of blank spines and the bare tract are plainly

## 2. The turn to the audience

The turn passes.

- It is Larry's, in the house cast id, and no real account says it. `turn-you`, "That's you,
  simcluster.", names the community before the first "you" of the invitation. The cluster's
  lights are what the tract faces, so every "you" after that is the community.
- "You make this show" is in the sense the sheet gives. The dive has just shown each level made
  from the one before, down to the community's posts.
- `turn-point` ("From now on, you can point.") and `turn-where` ("Then we look where you point.")
  are the sheet's "tagging the show's account ... guides where the show looks", said to members,
  whose tags the sheet says are read.
- `turn-look` ("That's all. No reply. No promise.") makes a tag a look and nothing more. It
  promises no place, use, puppet, reply, tag-back or message.
- There is no "anyone", "everyone" or "whoever". Nothing gives weight to tagging more often. Nothing
  says anything is in the lane, and nothing says anything on the show came from a tag.
- The picture keeps to the terms. The post box is the show's own drawing: no name, an empty ring
  for a picture, no words. Nothing appears under the handle. The tract leans toward the lights, not
  the box. The box goes out as the camera leaves, so no picture carries the tag up the pipeline
  into the show.
- bisks' suggestions and the owner's posts are not used, so the invitation cannot be staged as an
  answer to them.
- The handle is exactly `@latecluster.simcluster.tv`, typed and spoken (`turn-handle`).
- **note** (for the writer). The caption of `turn-handle` reads "@latecluster.simcluster.tv. No
  spaces." The full stop after "tv" is sentence punctuation. Someone copying from a still could
  take it as part of the address. The typed handle in the post box is exact, so this is only a
  note. A caption that ends the handle with no full stop would remove it.
- **note** (for the sound editor). `fit.json` is from the first version and has none of the
  redraft's new lines, including `turn-handle`. Whether the ear hears the handle as the address
  has not been checked. The terms require it "spoken ... exactly". Fit it before selection and
  report what is heard.
