# Audit: riemann-breaking-news against its terms

Brief: `briefs/screen/auditor-riemann-breaking.md`. Read against `research/terms-riemann.md` (the
combined segment's section and "Rules for the whole subject"), `research/pronouns.md`, the two kept
cards, the draft's `script.js`, `notes.json`, `sources.json` and `cast.js`, and the constitution.
Every text below was compared with the kept card by address.

Verdict: **change**

## must

- **must** (to the writer). `larry-banner` ("I had a banner."), with its cue `no-banner` and the
  laugh after it. It is a house remark on a member's market post, and the terms allow a house line
  near the market jokes only to introduce one ("who posted, and whether it was a post of their own, a
  reply or a quote"). It comes straight after norvid's "this is how i learned the riemann news lol"
  over the chart, and it only makes sense if the chart *is* the news. The anchor's banner (the
  announcement) has been beaten by a share-price chart. That takes the members' premise (the price
  moved because of the announcement) and builds on it as fact in the house voice, which the section
  "The market jokes without the exchange" forbids ("never says it, agrees with it, builds on it as a
  fact"). It also puts the news frame and the chart together in words, right after the frames have
  been kept apart, so the anchor is in effect captioning the chart with the announcement. Cut
  `larry-banner`, `no-banner` and the laugh beat that follows. Nothing replaces them. Whether
  norvid's card stays depends on the next `must`.
- **must** (to the project manager, for a terms ruling, or else to the writer). `card-norvid`
  puts davidcrespo's chart card, image included, inside norvid's card (`3n2qybgu3ts2q`). The terms
  say the chart image "may still be shown, **only** inside davidcrespo's card (`3n2qvzbb7dk2z`) …
  and inside moultano's card as the quoted post". norvid's card is not one of them, so the draft
  shows the chart in a third place the terms do not allow. The table does list `3n2qybgu3ts2q` for
  this segment and records that it quotes the chart post, so this may be a gap in the terms rather
  than in the draft. That is not mine to settle here. There are two ways to clear it:
  (a) the auditor who owns `research/terms-riemann.md` adds norvid's `3n2qybgu3ts2q` to the
  chart-image clause, with the same conditions (inside the card, as the quoted post, unaltered); or
  (b) the writer cuts `card-norvid`, `sibyl-norvid-intro` and `sibyl-norvid`, and removes norvid's
  account and post from `sources.json` and `featured` in `notes.json`. Showing norvid's card with the
  quoted post stripped of its image is not a third way: "this is how i learned" would then point at
  nothing, and the occasion would be lost (R4).
