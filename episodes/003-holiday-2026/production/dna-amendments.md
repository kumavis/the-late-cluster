# DNA amendments: 003-holiday-2026

Written at the final review of the holiday special, 27 November 2026, before anyone has watched
it with sound. Neither file of the constitution was touched.

## Experiment

What it was: four earnest minutes in Larry's own words, outside the studio, with no posts, no
guests and no audience. The premise chapter keeps sincerity to one short moment in the
community's words, earned by comedy; this special tests whether the show can hold a sincere
piece of its own without it becoming a speech. The owner set the form.

Its measure, from the two reviewers at select: shown once with sound to three people who have
not read it, at least two can say back the don't-have-to-finish, don't-get-to-quit idea in their
own words and what happened when he got home, at least two name something that happens and not
something they were told to feel, none can point at a line of Larry's own that told them how to
feel, none calls it a speech, a lesson or a list, and its cues hold no laugh, applause or sting.

Verdict before air: can't tell yet.

Why. It was built in whole, as set out. The one clause that can be read off the episode holds:
the cues are whoosh, tick, murmur and one gasp, and the only laugh is a character's voiced line.
Everything else in the measure needs people watching with sound, and nobody has: the cold viewers
are shown stills. On stills they named the place and the man in every set, the sign changing, the
door opening and a meal being served, and they missed two pictures that carry meaning (the figure
at the top window; six people looking at the viewer). The special also changes several things at
once (no posts, no guests, no audience, a new place, four minutes of sincerity), so even a clear
result will say whether the whole held and not which change did it. Scorecard row B12 is 1.

## Edits to the DNA in this pull request

none: it is one special, it has not aired, and nobody has yet watched it with sound, so nothing
it tried is evidence for a craft rule. The premise chapter's rules for sincerity stand as they
are for a weekly episode.

## Considered and not made

- **Sincerity** (`01-premise.md`). The special sets every one of the chapter's five conditions
  aside, by the owner's outline. If it airs and holds, what it shows is that a special can be
  made, not that a weekly episode may be sincere for longer or in its own words. No change now,
  and a slow one later.
- **A scorecard for a special** (`10-process.md`). The card was written for a weekly episode.
  This review says in its own text how it applied the card: gates with nothing to judge, rows
  the owner set aside scored for what they are for, the standing-segments row left out and the
  total taken out of 24. If a second special is made, that belongs in the chapter. One is not
  enough to write it from.
- **The one-page summary's list of out-of-bounds subjects** (`README.md`, "Never break these").
  The special's first briefs took their list from that line, which does not name religion, and
  the conflict with chapter 9's R10 was found only at the final review of the script. The line
  is a pointer to R10 and not the rule. Whether it should name more of R10's list is a small
  change the project manager can put to the owner; a brief should cite R10 itself in any case.
  Not made here: it is a summary of the constitution, and one brief's slip is thin evidence.
- **Cold viewers and things that move** (the workflow, not the bible). Stills cannot show a
  change of pace or a look, and a set of stills should span a cause and its effect: the set for
  the door held one frame with the figure in the window and four without. A note for whoever
  next chooses the sets.
- **Words the engine cannot say.** "walk", "they'd", "thy", "thou" and "ruling" are in the voice
  research file, where they belong. The voices chapter needs nothing.
