## 4. What pauses could not fix

- There is no street bed in the library, so every outdoor silence above is digital silence.
  Heard without pictures, the holds (snow 4.4 s, after the Avot quotation 3.4 s) may read as
  dead air. A builder's quiet outdoor bed (wind, far traffic, footsteps) would carry them.
- `murmur` is the only room sound: the restaurant, the apartment window, the door opening and
  the table all use it, and the table-wide "cut off on one breath" cannot be heard as a cut,
  only as the sample ending.
- `buzzer` still sounds like a game-show wrong answer at 0.35, as the writer feared.
- The busker is still `hy`, who speaks at the table a minute later.

## 5. Tricks

The script had no negative `wait` or `at`. I added one: the goodnight murmur at `at: 2.4` with
a `wait: -2.4` after it, because a positive `at` moves the cursor to the sound's start. The
timeline shows it doing what is wanted: the line 236.51 to 239.14, the murmur 237.51 to 240.39.

## 6. Worst words in the printed IPA

- Tirukkural: /tɪhɹukuɹʌɫ/, an audible h from the `Tih-` respelling.
- Tamil: /təmɪɫ/, first vowel reduced to a schwa.
- record (Eppy): /ɹɛkkɝd/, a doubled k; the known working spelling, left as is.
