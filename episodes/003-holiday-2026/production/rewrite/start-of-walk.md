# The start of the walk, three ways

For the owner's note of 27 November 2026 ("a little too much negativity, or focus on it ... a lot
is happening"). The stretch runs from the first words on the street to the restaurant. As staged
it is 50 words in 9 lines. `script.js` is untouched; the owner chooses first.

In all three:

- `how-have-you-been`, the unheard answer, `yeah-me-too`, `no-joke`, `i-looked`, `old-books`,
  `q-does-it-help` and `some-nights` are word for word as staged.
- `no-joke` with `i-looked` is kept, and still follows from the line before it.
- Nothing is named, and nothing new is drawn.
- One plain word is left for the half that is wrong, so that "What if none of it ever gets put
  right?" has something to point back to at the crossing: "awful" in A, "the worst" and
  "rattled" in B, "the bad way" in C. None of them answers it.

Words are counted as the brief counts them, by spaces.
