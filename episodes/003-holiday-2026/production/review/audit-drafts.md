## Conditions for the final script and the credits (not changes to these drafts)

- **C1. Name the translations.** special.md asks that a quotation come "from a named published
  translation". Writer-1 names Aston and Gorfinkle on screen. Writers 2 and 3 name the
  translators only in code comments, and writer-2 says nothing else is put on screen. Whatever
  is selected, the credits must name every translation quoted: Long 1862 (Marcus), Gorfinkle
  (Avot), Aston 1899 (Hōjōki), Bellows 1923 (Hávamál), as applicable. Owner: the project
  manager, at the final script and the build.
- **C2. Gorfinkle's year.** If a date is shown, 1913 is the first publication; the verified
  words are from the undated second edition (card, "How sure"). Stating "Gorfinkle, 1913" is
  normal citation; nothing further is asserted.
- **C3. The final audit re-checks** any line the final writer adds near `q-put-right`
  (writer-2) or `fast` / `fast-lately` (all drafts), the places nearest the owner's "justice
  seems rare".

## Outside this audit (for the reviewers, not a verdict)

The three drafts, written independently, share several lines and devices almost word for word:
"And fair is hard to find" (all three); Tarfon with the card's exact safe wording (all three);
a gold scarf (all three); Eppy calling a "record" or "house record" (all three); Sibyl stating a
"house rule" or "the rule" (all three); a red crossing on an empty road with "I know. I wait
anyway" or close to it (all three); "Some wrote down what helped" (writers 2 and 3). This
audit does not judge repetition; it is noted so the reviewers with the record of what has aired
can.
