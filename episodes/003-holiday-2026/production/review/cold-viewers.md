# Cold viewers, episode three

Six viewers were each shown the still frames of one stretch, at 640 by 360, and nothing else:
no sound, no script, no other set. Each was asked what the thing on screen is and what
happened. The frames are in `shots/003-holiday-2026/_cold/set-<n>/` and the moments each set
was taken at are in `review/cold/sets.json`. The frames were shot after the scenes were
joined and before the frame audit's changes.

The pipeline's tool makes these sets from stretches an experiment names; this special was
made by hand, so the project manager chose the stretches: the ones that carry meaning without
a word. Two things limit what the answers show. Stills cannot show speed, so "the cook slows
while the room hurries" cannot be tested this way. And a caption stays on screen for about
half a second after its line ends: sets 4 and 5 each caught one, so those two viewers saw a
few of Larry's words.

## What the answers show

- **Read as meant:** the place and the man in every set; the sign changing and the crossing
  (set 3); the door opening by itself and its light on the pavement (set 4); a meal being
  served (set 6).
- **Misread: the work lamp is taken for a microphone** (set 1). The opening's point is that
  the show is off, and a host at a microphone says the opposite.
- **Not seen: the snow** (set 3). Nothing in the answer mentions it, at the size most viewers
  will watch.
- **Not seen: the figure at the window** (set 4). The viewer saw the door open and gave no
  cause for it. The figure is in the first frame and gone from the rest.
- **Read as a screen: the kitchen hatch** (set 2), "a chef on a screen at the back".
- **Not seen: that the six are looking at us, and that a chair arrives** (set 5). The viewer
  saw empty chairs and a place setting. Stills are weak evidence for a look.

These went to the builders with the frame audit's findings. What stills cannot settle is for
the owner's watch.
