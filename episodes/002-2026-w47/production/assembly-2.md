## Where the energy rises and rests

The plotter starts low and warm. The account bit is a dry ten seconds. The interview climbs
through three answers to a big laugh at 2:04, and the news takes it from there to the pile and
applause at 3:34: the first peak. The making-of is the rest, down to the invitation at the centre
and back up to a burp. Eppy restarts it at once at 5:17. The coffee segment drops into its
silence, then climbs to the second big laugh and applause at 7:21. The song is gentle and ends
warm. The chess closes on the lap, "I resign." and the long applause. No two neighbours look
alike: desk and paper, desk alone, desk and couch, news set, the inside of the host, sports desk,
a game on a panel, the band, three at a table.

## The account bit: before the making-of

- **It comes first because it is the earlier fact.** "I finally got an account" is news only until
  the viewer has been told what the account is for. After the making-of it would repeat the
  handle with the terms taken off, and the bare handle would be the last thing said about it.
  This way the last thing said about the account is the making-of's "No reply. No promise."
- **The handle is familiar when it is typed.** It is seen once, large, for about six seconds,
  while a line under it makes the viewer read it end to end. Nearly four minutes later the
  making-of types it a character at a time, and the viewer recognises it.
- **They do not say the same thing.** The bit says there is an account and shows the handle. It
  says nothing about tagging, pointing, following or replying, and asks nothing of the viewer.
  The handle is not read aloud, not typed, and does not turn the colour a mention turns; all of
  that is the making-of's. The making-of calls the handle "the show's name", which is the bit's
  joke from the other side.
- **Not adjacent.** Two segments of other people's work are between them.

  in long, the bit is the first thing to take out: nothing else refers to it.
- **Three names that sound alike.** David (interview, song), davidcrespo (news) and Dave (coffee).
  In this order the interview and the news are neighbours: "Please welcome David", and a little
  over a minute later "davidcrespo posted a chart." The interview is first so that its David is
  established before the other appears, and each has his own puppet, chyron and handle. If a
