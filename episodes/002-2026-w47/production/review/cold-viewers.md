# The experiment's second question: cold viewers

The experiment's measure asks that a cold viewer, given only a segment's wordless stretch, write one
sentence on what the thing is and what happened; the stretch worked if that sentence names the turn
its writer named beforehand.

How it was run (by the project manager, 2026-11-20). For each of the three segments the longest
interval with no spoken line and no audience sound was measured from the rendered `cues.json`. One
frame per second across it was shot from the staged segment and copied under neutral file names.
Each set went to a separate viewer that had read no script, card, note or brief and was told only
that these were stills, one second apart, from a television show in which nobody speaks.

Limits. The viewers saw stills, not motion, and had no sound, so anything a stretch says with its
beat or its timing was not tested. The viewers were a small model, not people. The owner's own cold
watch of the video is the better test.

Was it run: yes in all three (each interval is 10.0 s or more, by the cues).
Did it work: the turn was named for two of the three stretches.
