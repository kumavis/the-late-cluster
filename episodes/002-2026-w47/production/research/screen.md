## For every kept card

1. **No bio has been read.** Research had no network, so the only bio check so far is the scraper's profile filter. R11 and R14 need the bio of every account that reaches a script. Until that is done, nobody on these cards is cleared on that point.
2. **Most pictures and videos have not been seen.** Where a card says an image or video has no alt text, nobody knows what it shows. Do not describe it, redraw it or joke about it as if seen, and do not build a segment on showing it until someone has looked (R15, R5). The cards say which these are.
3. **A redacted record is never a source.** Nothing is taken from a post the digest marks as redacted, and no fact that exists only in such a post is stated.
4. **A thread with replies withheld under the consent rules is never presented as a whole conversation.** Single posts and pairs from it may be used as the posts they were. Counts the digest gives for it may be said.
5. **Names, not pronouns.** The pronouns on the cards are the researchers'. Scripts refer to real people by display name or handle and do not gender anyone (R1, R10).
6. **People outside the cluster's membership** (marked on the cards) appear only as the occasion of a member's or maker's answer, and are not cast as puppets (R7).
7. **Small accounts** appear once, as the hero of one moment, and are not sent an audience for anything else they posted (R7).
8. **Words nobody could explain** stay unexplained and carry no punchline (R15): "catoid", "#NaNoFoMo", "claim" as a caption, "a jev", "guava combat", "bisk", "ghosts and ninjas", "Moebius" in the polyhedra video's first request, "spin can".
