# Media notes: what the unseen pictures and pages show

Research could not see images, videos or pages without alt text. The project manager opened the
posts and pages below in a browser on 2026-11-19 (UTC) and wrote down plainly what is visible.
These are descriptions for writers and builders to work from. They are not sources: nothing here
is a quote, and anything shown on screen must still match the post it comes from.

## The pen plotter (`made-pen-plotter-replies`, `voices-plotter-queue-small-accounts`)

- `…/samantha.wiki/post/3n25u7fw3zs2q` (video, an earlier post about the machine): a small desktop
  CNC router with a blue printed pen holder moving over a white sheet on a gridded bed. The post
  says it is a Genmitsu 3018 with a pen plotter attachment and a blue Pilot G2 0.7 mm pen, plotting
  a space-filling curve.
- `…/samantha.wiki/post/3n2cc5mbfqs22` (the "experiment is live" post): a camera looking straight
  down at a blank white sheet held at its corners on the plotter's bed.
- `…/samantha.wiki/post/3n2chz7spq22k` ("The queue is half full"): the same sheet with roughly its
  top third covered in rows of small blue lettering, each row running the full width of the page.
- `…/samantha.wiki/post/3n2cpdy5tbs2k` ("Replies are locked"): the sheet nearly full. About forty
  tightly packed lines of small blue single-stroke lettering in one justified block. Replies run
  on one after another, each starting with its author's handle and separated by `//`. Below the
  block, at the foot of the page, is an empty ruled rectangle.
- The picture to stage is therefore: one pen, one sheet, small blue block lettering filling the
  page line by line from the top, replies run together with `//` between them, and a box left
  empty at the bottom.

  "A small moment of stillness. Stay a little while. Warm coffee. Polyhedral sakura trees and
  animated instruments playing さくら さくら across the valley." with a "Begin morning" button; the
  controls "WASD to walk · Drag to look · Space to sip · 1–5 to visit a spot · P to hide controls ·
  Q to hide mug", and a prompt "E Refill Coffee from Kettle". The 3D scene did not draw in the

  pink blossom trees built from small polyhedra, a hanging string of red diamond shapes at the
