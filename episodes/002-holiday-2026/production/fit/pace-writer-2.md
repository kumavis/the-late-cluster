## 4. What pauses could not fix

- **Footsteps.** The comments ask for two sets of footsteps under the walk that stop whenever they stop. No beat makes them, so the walking silences are true silence. That is a job for a builder or for a sfx beat.
- **The busker reprise.** The comment at walk-past asks for the phrase again, low and panned left, under the next three lines. There is no beat for it, and I could not add one.
- **Murmur beds are about 3 s long.** The restaurant has three, at 75.4, 83.5 and 99.4 s, with gaps between them. At the table the comment says "keep it as a bed to the end", but there are only two murmurs. I put the second at the end, so the owner's three lines play in a quiet room with the bed fading in only on "Goodnight". A bed that loops or runs long is a builder's job.
- **Speaking rate.** The writer's rates are kept. Larry speaks at about 176 wpm against the usual 159. The pauses carry the slowness, not the lines. If the owner hears him as quick, the fix is a rate of about 0.92 on his longer lines, the writer's call.
