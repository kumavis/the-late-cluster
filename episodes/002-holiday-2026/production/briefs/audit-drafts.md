- **Role:** auditor (spawn the `auditor` subagent defined in .claude/agents/auditor.md)

- **Write:** `episodes/002-holiday-2026/production/review/audit-drafts.md`. Nothing else.

No network is needed and none is used: you check the drafts against the research file.

## The task

For each of the three drafts, go through every spoken line and every cue comment and report:

1. **Every attribution.** Each place Larry names, or plainly points at, a thinker, a text, a
   tradition or a people. For each: the line id, the card it rests on, and one of
   - `ok`: the wording is one the card calls safe and the idea is told as the card tells it;
   - `fix`: say exactly what is wrong (a legendary figure spoken of as historical; a book's
     saying put in a person's mouth; a detail of someone's life the card does not give; a
     telling that says more, or something other, than the source does; a card's trap used);
   - `not on the shelf`: the idea, saying or name is not in the research file at all.
2. **Every quotation.** Each line presented as someone's words. Compare it letter for letter
   with the card's verified quotation. `exact`, or show the difference. A quotation whose
   card says "no verified quotation" is a `fix`. Words presented as a quotation that are
   really the writer's telling are a `fix`.
3. **Anything invented and presented as fact**: an anecdote, a date, a number, a saying
   credited to "the ancients" or to a people at large.
4. **Out of bounds** (R10): health, grief, identity, politics, jobs, conflict. Any named or
   hinted event, person, party, country, law or side. Quote the line. Larry's paycheck line
   is the owner's own and is not a finding.
5. **Respect:** anything a community that holds one of these texts as scripture would
   reasonably mind: a joke at its expense, a ranking, Larry speaking for it.
6. **The fixed points:** the holiday named, or its food; the viewer given a voice; anything
   said after the owner's last line; a reference to an earlier episode that you can see from
   the script alone.

Give each draft a verdict: `pass`, or `changes` with the list. Say exactly what must change
and never how to rewrite it. You do not judge whether a draft is good.

## Always

- The constitution is the owner's: never change it. You change no script.
- You work alone, from this brief and the files it names. When you finish, reply in two or
  three sentences: the verdict per draft and the most serious finding.
